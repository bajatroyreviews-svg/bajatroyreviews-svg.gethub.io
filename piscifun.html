The opening word

Now, I'll tell you what — there are a whole lot of fishing companies out there, and plenty of folks will try to sell you a story before they ever tell you what their gear can do. But every once in a while, you run across a company that understands something simple: regular folks ought to be able to get solid fishing gear, get out on the water, and make some memories without having to sell the truck to afford it.

That is where Piscifun comes in.

Piscifun is a fishing-gear company built around a straightforward idea: make dependable, practical gear that anglers can afford and actually put to use. And I am proud to represent them.


Mountain man & the salt

Now, I am a mountain man at heart. Give me a backwoods trail, a cold creek, a remote tributary, a pair of waders, and a fishing hole far enough off the road that you have to earn your way into it, and I am a happy man.

But I will not lie to you — I love the saltwater too. There is something about standing near the ocean with wind in your face and salt in your beard that wakes up a different part of a man.

“Since I was a kid, I have had that old dream of setting out to sea under the Jolly Roger. And between you and me, I damn near need that eye patch these days. So I reckon I will keep one eye on the horizon and the other on Davy Jones' Locker. A pirate can dream, can't he?”

— Baja Troy


Chapter one

From Backwoods Water to Blue Water

Whether you are fishing a quiet mountain stream, casting a lake bank, working a river, heading for the coast, fishing the surf, going after inshore species, or looking farther offshore — Piscifun has gear made for different styles of fishing and different types of anglers.

One day, I might be walking into a cold creek wearing waders, trying not to leave size-16 tracks big enough to get mistaken for Bigfoot. The next, I may be down at the coast with salt spray on my face, trying to remember whether I have enough sea legs to stay above the water instead of ending up as a story told by Davy Jones himself.

No matter which direction I am headed, I want gear that makes sense for the job.


Chapter two

Why I Like Their Rods

I love a good fishing pole, especially when it has a purpose behind it. A mountain creek does not ask the same thing from you as a beach, a pier, a bay, or deep water.

Piscifun understands that difference. Their freshwater rod selection includes casting, spinning, and fly-rod options, while their saltwater lineup reaches into heavier applications for surf, jigging, trolling, inshore, offshore, and deep-sea fishing.

  • Beginner? Start building a setup without being overwhelmed
  • Seasoned? Find something that fits your technique and your water
  • Want it matched? Rod-and-reel combos, freshwater and saltwater

“That gives anglers choices. And I like companies that give people choices instead of acting like every fisherman, every fish, and every body of water is the same.”


Chapter three

Reels That Fit the Adventure

A reel is not just a shiny little machine sitting on top of a rod. It is the part that has to keep doing its job when the fish finally decides to make things interesting.

Piscifun carries spinning reels, baitcasting reels, and electric reels — from lighter freshwater fishing to heavier saltwater and deep-water applications.

  • AtlantiX 12000 — big-game deep-sea electric reel, aluminum build, smart LCD display, stated max drag of 110 pounds
  • Saltflow rods — built for offshore saltwater, designed to pair with the Kraken X electric reel

You put a deep-sea rod in my hand, point me toward blue water, and tell me there might be something big pulling below the surface — well, I am going to pay attention. That is when the old pirate in me starts waking up. Just keep me out of Davy Jones' Locker long enough to tell the story afterward.


Chapter four

The Company Behind the Gear

Piscifun was founded by Ben and Peter, two friends brought together by their passion for fishing and family. The company's familiar message, "Fish with Family," says a lot about the kind of spirit they want connected to the brand.

Fishing is not always about the biggest fish, the fanciest boat, or the most expensive gear in the world. A whole lot of the best fishing memories happen with a friend, a kid, a parent, a grandparent, or somebody who simply needed a day by the water.

It is a first cast. A first fish. Coffee before daylight. A tackle bag in the truck. A story that gets bigger every time it is told. It is getting outside when life has been too loud.

“Piscifun has built its brand around making fishing gear more accessible. That is a mission I can respect.”

Chapter five

Why I Represent Piscifun

I am Baja Troy. I am a backwoods man, a mountain wanderer, a fisherman, a gear tester, and a storyteller. I go after wild places — cold water, remote tributaries, long trails, campfire smoke, and the kind of adventures that leave dirt on your boots and a good story in your pocket.
