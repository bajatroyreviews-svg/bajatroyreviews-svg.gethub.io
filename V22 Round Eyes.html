BAJA TROY CHECKS IT OUT

A Little Bang for Your Night

by Baja Troy Reviews
Inside Round Eyes Fireworks in Lake Havasu, Arizona
FIRST LOOK

A Shop That Stacks Up

Baja Troy is 6'6" — a little taller with the cowboy hat on — and the shelves at Round Eyes Fireworks stand right up there with him.

I walked into a bright, well-lit, windowed shop and got greeted right away by a gentleman. The first thing I noticed was the size of the place and the product packed wall to wall. I'm 6'6", and a little taller with my cowboy hat on, and those shelves were up there with me.

Everything looked organized, colorful, and easy to look through. There was plenty on the shelves, plus even more product behind the counter. It had that feeling of walking into a candy store — except you are a grown adult, the shelves are towering over you, and the candy happens to sparkle.

  • Bright, windowed store
  • Shelves reaching tall
  • Product organized wall to wall
  • More selection behind the counter
  • Friendly welcome when you walk in

THE GOODS

Bright Boxes and Safety Yeller

Colorful shelves and safety-yeller items give the store a bright, high-energy look.

The color hits you right away. Bright boxes, different sizes, and displays everywhere give Round Eyes Fireworks a real specialty-shop feel. You can walk around, look up, look down, and keep finding more to check out.

One thing I liked was the safety yeller gear and the cool logo shirts. The shirts looked sharp, and I dig the logo. I could see people wanting to walk out with some Round Eyes gear along with whatever they came in for. Every good local business needs something people can wear out the door and represent.

BAJA TROY'S TAKE

Worth a Walk Up the Strip

Round Eyes Fireworks gives Lake Havasu a bright, local place to browse before a celebration.

Round Eyes Fireworks is a cool little area to stop into while you are walking up and down the strip in Lake Havasu. You can check out the shop, take a look at the selection, talk to the people behind the counter, and see what catches your eye.

The place has a friendly feel, a serious amount of product, and that grown-up kid-in-a-candy-store excitement if you have always been into fireworks. If you are in Lake Havasu, go check it out.
