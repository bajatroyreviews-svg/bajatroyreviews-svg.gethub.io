HOW TO USE THIS PACK

Down in San Diego County, the lakes sit like a string of blue beads across the hills—city parks, county reservoirs, and a few quiet holes where a man can get away from the noise and remember why he started fishin’ in the first place. These waters ain’t as high and cold as the mountain lakes, but they got bass, cats, trout in the cool months, and enough panfish to keep the young’uns busy. If you’re willin’ to pay the day fee, mind the heat, and cast where the shade hits, these lakes’ll put dinner on the table and a few good stories in your pocket.

DIXON LAKE

ESCONDIDO · SAN DIEGO COUNTY
Out near Escondido, where the hills roll green in a good year and the catfish act like they own the bottom.
SPECIES
catfishbasstroutbluegillcrappie
ACCESS
bank-friendlyboat-goodkayak-good
VIBE
city lakefamilytrophy potentialheat-heavy
DIXON LAKE

ACCESS & FEES

DAY USE
Day fishing fee typically in the $20–$30 range (check current City of Escondido rates).
LAUNCH
Small boats and kayaks allowed; launch fees may apply—check current rules.
ACCESS
Shore fishing all around, small boat ramp, kayak-friendly.
HOURS
Daylight hours; gates close at dusk.
CLOSURES
Can close for maintenance, water work, or special events.
PERMIT
California sport fishing license required; special regulations may apply—check CDFW and lake postings.
DIXON LAKE

SPECIES & STOCKING

PRIMARY
Channel catfish, largemouth bass
SECONDARY
Rainbow trout (seasonal), bluegill, crappie
STOCKING
Regular catfish and trout stockings; bass self-sustaining.
SIZE
Bass commonly 2–5 lbs, with trophies over 6–8 lbs possible; catfish 5–15 lbs, with bigger ones out there; trout 12–18″ typical.
DIXON LAKE

SEASONS & TIMING

BEST MONTHS
March–June, September–November
BEST TIME OF DAY
Early morning and evening for bass; night for catfish in summer; midday for trout in winter.
SEASONAL PATTERNS
SPRING
Bass shallow for spawn; cats active.
SUMMER
Bass deeper; cats more active at night; trout sparse.
FALL
Bass and cats feed hard as water cools.
WINTER
Trout focus; bass and cats slower but still catchable.
DIXON LAKE

TACTICS & GEAR

TOP TECHNIQUES
  • Catfish: Bottom fishing with cut bait, nightcrawlers, stink bait.
  • Bass: Drop shot, Carolina rig, jigs, crankbaits, topwater early/late.
  • Trout: Still-fishing with bait, small jigs, trolling light lures in season.
BAITS & LURES
Catfish: Cut bait, nightcrawlers, prepared catfish baits. · Bass: Soft plastics (worms, creatures), jigs, drop-shot rigs, crankbaits, topwater. · Trout: Nightcrawlers, PowerBait, small spinners/spoons.
ROD & LINE SETUPS
Catfish: 7′ medium-heavy to heavy, 15–25 lb test. · Bass: 6′6″–7′ medium to medium-heavy, 10–17 lb test. · Trout: 6′–6′6″ light to medium-light, 4–8 lb test.
DIXON LAKE

FACILITIES & SAFETY

RESTROOMS & SHADE
Good facilities around main areas; shade structures and picnic tables available.
RAMPS & RENTALS
Small ramp; rentals may be available—check current offerings.
CELL SERVICE
Fair to good in most areas.
SAFETY NOTES
  • Summer heat is no joke—water, shade, and sunscreen are mandatory, not optional.
  • Afternoon winds can whip the lake up; respect the chop in small boats.
  • Watch for submerged structure and changing water levels.
DIXON LAKE

MOUNTAIN MAN VERDICT

4.5 / 5

Intermediate (solid cats and bass; trout add variety in cool months)

Spring mornings for bass, summer nights for catfish, and cooler months for trout—pick your poison and plan accordingly.
