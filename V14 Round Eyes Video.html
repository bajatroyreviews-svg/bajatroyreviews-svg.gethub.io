Inside Round Eyes Fireworks in Lake Havasu, Arizona
BAJA TROY REVIEWS
FIRST LOOK

A Shop That Stacks Up

The shelves at Round Eyes Fireworks stand right up there with Baja Troy.

Bright, windowed shop — product organized wall to wall, plenty more behind the counter, and a friendly welcome when you walk in.

THE GOODS

Bright Boxes and Safety Yeller

Colorful shelves and safety-yeller items give the store a high-energy look.

Bright boxes, displays everywhere, safety yeller gear, and cool logo shirts you will want to wear out the door.

BAJA TROY'S TAKE

Worth a Walk Up the Strip

A bright, local place to browse before a celebration.

Friendly feel, a serious amount of product, and that grown-up kid-in-a-candy-store excitement. If you are in Lake Havasu, go check it out.

FUSE & PYRO

Fuse, Pyro & Party Supplies

Fuse and pyro supplies, gender-reveal items, and Round Eyes gear line the back wall.

The back wall covers the hobby side of things — fuse, pyro supplies, and fun extras like gender-reveal confetti cannons.

BAJA TROY SAYS

“I'm six-foot-six, a little taller with my cowboy hat on, and them shelves were right up there with me.”

“I like the shirts, I dig the logo, and that safety yeller stands out.”

“Make sure you're walking up and down the strip, then stop in and check this place out.”
