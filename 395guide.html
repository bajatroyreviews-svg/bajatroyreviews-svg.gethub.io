NOTES & TIPSSmall forest-service lake with easy shore access and light pressure. Spring plants are your best shot at trout — fish 4–6 lb line and keep presentations subtle in the clear water.

BIG BEAR LAKE

BIG BEAR CITY · SAN BERNARDINO MOUNTAINS
rainbow troutlargemouth & smallmouth basschannel catfishbluegill
BEST SEASONspring & fall for trout; summer for bass; catfish all season
BAIT & GEARpowerBait, worms, and shrimp for trout; jig-spinners, crankbaits, and soft plastics for bass; stink bait for catfish
NOTES & TIPSBig open water — afternoon wind builds fast, so fish mornings. A boat or kayak unlocks the best trolling; marinas rent by the hour. Catfish slide into the inlets after dark.

MAMMOTH LAKES · LONG VALLEY

OWENS RIVER SYSTEM

UPPER OWENS RIVER

BIG SPRINGS → CROWLEY LAKE · LONG VALLEY
rainbow troutbrown trout
BEST SEASONspring spawn (mar–jun) and fall (sep–feb); summer hatches
BAIT & GEARnymphs, caddis, and dry flies; worms and small spinners also produce
NOTES & TIPSLegendary pre-spawn staging near benton crossing in march–april — that stretch is catch-and-release, check current regs. Easy wading, but spring mosquitos are fierce. Late-season fish stack in the deeper bends.

PLEASANT VALLEY RESERVOIR

BISHOP · LOWER OWENS HEADWATERS
rainbow troutbass
BEST SEASONspring through fall; best while plants are fresh
BAIT & GEARlight spinners, worms, and small crankbaits; float-tube fly setups
NOTES & TIPSSmall reservoir right off 395 — shore and float tube both work. Weeds take over by late summer, so fish the edges early. Drawdowns push trout onto the old creek channel; look for the drop.

LOWER OWENS RIVER

BISHOP · MEADOW STRETCH PARALLEL TO 395
rainbow troutbrown trout
BEST SEASONyear-round; late fall and early spring are most productive
BAIT & GEARnymphs and dries — adams, BWO, PMD; worms and small spinners from the bank
NOTES & TIPSMiles of public meadow water via dirt pullouts along 395. The winter midge bite is famous — size 20–22 bugs on 6x or finer. Afternoon wind is the norm; be on the water by 8 am.

OWENS RIVER — BIG PINE TO LONE PINE

SOUTHERN OWENS VALLEY
rainbow troutlargemouth bass
BEST SEASONspring through fall; trout at dawn & dusk, bass in midday heat
BAIT & GEARspinnerbaits, jigs, and soft plastics for bass; worms and spinners for trout
NOTES & TIPSThe southern stretch warms fast — below pleasant valley it becomes bass water. Irrigation flows move the fish; scout current seams and undercut banks. Rattlesnake country in the brush — watch your step.

GRANT LAKE

LEE VINING · JUNE LAKE LOOP NORTH END
rainbow troutmackinaw (lake trout)
BEST SEASONsummer through early fall
BAIT & GEARtrolling spoons and dodgers for mackinaw; spinners and worms for rainbows
NOTES & TIPSHigh-altitude reservoir with a rough access road — 4wd helps early season. Mackinaw hold deep: troll slow and low with lead-core or a downrigger. Wind rolls hard down the gorge most afternoons.

JUNE LAKE LOOP

JUNE LAKE · SCENIC LOOP OFF 395
rainbow troutbrown troutmackinaw
BEST SEASONjune–september; early-fall turn can be special
BAIT & GEARtrolling rigs, spinners, and worms; family-friendly shore setups
NOTES & TIPSFour fishable lakes on one 16-mile loop — june, gull, silver, and grant — with roadside pullouts on all of them. Resort towns on the loop stock tackle, bait, and boat rentals.

BENTON CROSSING & CUTOFF CREEKS

BENTON · HOT CREEK AREA
wild rainbow trout

BAIT & GEARultralight rods and small spinners; dry flies on long 5x leaders
NOTES & TIPSSpring-creek character — clear, slow, spooky fish. Go long and light, and match tiny midges and baetis. Several stretches border working ranches along benton crossing road; stay in the public corridor.

NOTES & TIPSWarm-water trickles and ranch ponds — a fun contrast to the trout water and rarely crowded. Ask before fishing private ponds; most owners are friendly. Overcast days fish best here.

BEST SEASONspring & fall; summer mornings and evenings
BAIT & GEARnymphs, caddis, stoneflies, and PMDs; ultralight spinners or worms

EAST FORK SIDE CHANNELS & TRIBUTARIES

CARSON VALLEY · EAST FORK OXBOWS

BEST SEASONspring & fall

WADSWORTH · PYRAMID LAKE PAIUTE TRIBE

BEST SEASONspring & fall; early season right after plants
BAIT & GEARtrolling spoons and dodgers; powerBait-style baits from shore

SPOONER LAKE

LAKE TAHOE EAST SHORE · NEAR SPARKS
rainbow troutsmallmouth bass
BEST SEASONspring through fall
BAIT & GEARultralight spinning gear, small spinners and worms; light fly rod
NOTES & TIPSState park access with a day-use fee; hike-in shoreline, no gas motors. Smallmouth cruise the shallows on calm mornings. Best before the summer beach crowds arrive.
