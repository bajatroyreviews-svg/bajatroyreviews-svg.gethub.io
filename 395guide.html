ARROW BEAR · SAN BERNARDINO COUNTY

ARROWBEAR LAKE

  • rainbow trout
  • largemouth bass
  • panfish
BEST SEASON — spring through early fall; heaviest with spring CDFW plants

BAIT & GEAR. powerBait, worms, and small spinners for trout; light jigs and spinners for bass

NOTES & TIPS
Small forest-service lake with easy shore access and light pressure. Spring plants are your best shot at trout — fish 4–6 lb line and keep presentations subtle in the clear water.
BIG BEAR CITY · SAN BERNARDINO MOUNTAINS

BIG BEAR LAKE

  • rainbow trout
  • largemouth & smallmouth bass
  • channel catfish
  • bluegill
BEST SEASON — spring & fall for trout; summer for bass; catfish all season

BAIT & GEAR. powerBait, worms, and shrimp for trout; jig-spinners, crankbaits, and soft plastics for bass; stink bait for catfish

NOTES & TIPS
Big open water — afternoon wind builds fast, so fish mornings. A boat or kayak unlocks the best trolling; marinas rent by the hour. Catfish slide into the inlets after dark.

MAMMOTH LAKES · LONG VALLEY

CROWLEY LAKE

  • rainbow trout
  • brown trout
  • cutthroat trout
BEST SEASON — may–october peak; winter still works but can be brutal

BAIT & GEAR. fly rigs — streamers, nymphs, and dry flies; trolling spoons & dodgers; worms and powerBait

NOTES & TIPS
A boat or float tube is nearly essential — shore access is limited. Famous for the fall perch-fry hatch; match tiny. Wind stacks up quick on the west side; north end near the inlet turns on in fall.
ROCK CREEK ROAD · NORTH OF MAMMOTH

ROCK CREEK & TWIN LAKES

  • rainbow trout
  • brown trout
  • smallmouth bass
BEST SEASON — late spring through fall; excellent early fall dry-fly bite

BAIT & GEAR. jigs, light spinners, and small crankbaits; nymphs and terrestrials on the fly rod

NOTES & TIPS
Roadside access right off 395. Lower Twin Lake has a resort with boat rentals; campgrounds fill on summer weekends. Browns push shallow at dusk once September cools the water.
CONVICT CREEK · SOUTH OF MAMMOTH

CONVICT LAKE

  • rainbow trout
  • brown trout
BEST SEASON — late spring through early fall

BAIT & GEAR. ultralight spinning gear, small spoons and spinners; worms and powerBait from shore

NOTES & TIPS
Stunning cirque lake with a flat shoreline loop — easy for all ages. Fall browns stage near the inlet. Wind funnels off the peaks, so go early; resort store at the north end has bait.
BIG PINE · OWENS VALLEY

TINEMAHA RESERVOIR

  • rainbow trout
  • largemouth bass
  • channel catfish
BEST SEASON — spring through fall; best right after early-spring plants

BAIT & GEAR. worms and powerBait-style baits for trout; jigs, crankbaits, and soft plastics for bass

NOTES & TIPS
Big desert reservoir where wind is the main hazard — fish the lee shore. Work the inlet after flow events and the 395 riprap for trout. A boat helps reach the better water; check levels, they swing hard.
LONE PINE · SOUTHERN OWENS VALLEY

LITTLE LAKE & BIG LAKE

  • rainbow trout
  • bass
  • panfish
BEST SEASON — spring through fall; early-season plants drive the bite

BAIT & GEAR. light spinners, worms, and powerBait; tiny jigs for panfish

NOTES & TIPS
Town lakes with easy shore access — great warm-up water before heading higher. Summer algae can slow the trout bite; in July and August fish dawn and dusk. Panfish keep kids busy all day.
BIG PINE · OWENS VALLEY

OWENS RIVER PONDS

  • rainbow trout
BEST SEASON — spring through early fall

BAIT & GEAR. worms, powerBait, and small spinners; simple shore setups

NOTES & TIPS
A string of small CDFW-stocked ponds right off 395 with mowed banks and easy parking — perfect for beginners. Heavy plant trucks roll through in spring; get there within a few days of a plant.

BIG SPRINGS → CROWLEY LAKE · LONG VALLEY

UPPER OWENS RIVER

  • rainbow trout
  • brown trout
BEST SEASON — spring spawn (mar–jun) and fall (sep–feb); summer hatches

BAIT & GEAR. nymphs, caddis, and dry flies; worms and small spinners also produce

NOTES & TIPS
Legendary pre-spawn staging near Benton Crossing in March–April — that stretch is catch-and-release, check current regs. Easy wading, but spring mosquitos are fierce. Late-season fish stack in the deeper bends.
BISHOP · LOWER OWENS HEADWATERS

PLEASANT VALLEY RESERVOIR

  • rainbow trout
  • bass
BEST SEASON — spring through fall; best while plants are fresh

BAIT & GEAR. light spinners, worms, and small crankbaits; float-tube fly setups

NOTES & TIPS
Small reservoir right off 395 — shore and float tube both work. Weeds take over by late summer, so fish the edges early. Drawdowns push trout onto the old creek channel; look for the drop.
BISHOP · MEADOW STRETCH PARALLEL TO 395

LOWER OWENS RIVER

  • rainbow trout
  • brown trout
BEST SEASON — year-round; late fall and early spring are most productive

BAIT & GEAR. nymphs and dries — adams, BWO, PMD; worms and small spinners from the bank

NOTES & TIPS
Miles of public meadow water via dirt pullouts along 395. The winter midge bite is famous — size 20–22 bugs on 6x or finer. Afternoon wind is the norm; be on the water by 8 am.
SOUTHERN OWENS VALLEY

OWENS RIVER — BIG PINE TO LONE PINE

  • rainbow trout
  • largemouth bass
BEST SEASON — spring through fall; trout at dawn & dusk, bass in midday heat

BAIT & GEAR. spinnerbaits, jigs, and soft plastics for bass; worms and spinners for trout

NOTES & TIPS
The southern stretch warms fast — below Pleasant Valley it becomes bass water. Irrigation flows move the fish; scout current seams and undercut banks. Rattlesnake country in the brush — watch your step.

LEE VINING · JUNE LAKE LOOP NORTH END

GRANT LAKE

  • rainbow trout
  • mackinaw (lake trout)
BEST SEASON — summer through early fall

BAIT & GEAR. trolling spoons and dodgers for mackinaw; spinners and worms for rainbows

NOTES & TIPS
High-altitude reservoir with a rough access road — 4wd helps early season. Mackinaw hold deep: troll slow and low with lead-core or a downrigger. Wind rolls hard down the gorge most afternoons.
JUNE LAKE · SCENIC LOOP OFF 395

JUNE LAKE LOOP

  • rainbow trout
  • brown trout
  • mackinaw
BEST SEASON — june–september; early-fall turn can be special

BAIT & GEAR. trolling rigs, spinners, and worms; family-friendly shore setups

NOTES & TIPS
Four fishable lakes on one 16-mile loop — June, Gull, Silver, and Grant — with roadside pullouts on all of them. Resort towns on the loop stock tackle, bait, and boat rentals.
JUNE LAKE LOOP · CARD 2 OF 2

JUNE LAKE LOOP — CONTINUED

  • mackinaw
  • brown trout
  • rainbow trout
BEST SEASON — summer trolling; fall creek-mouth staging for browns

BAIT & GEAR. downriggers or lead-core for macks; streamers at the inlets for browns

NOTES & TIPS
Silver Lake inlet is a fall brown magnet. Weekend parking fills by 9 am — beat the crowd. Afternoon wind on June Lake means anchoring up and switching to nymphs; Gull Lake warms first and can fish slow in August.
BENTON · HOT CREEK AREA

BENTON CROSSING & CUTOFF CREEKS

  • wild rainbow trout
BEST SEASON — summer and early fall, after snowmelt drops

BAIT & GEAR. ultralight rods and small spinners; dry flies on long 5x leaders

NOTES & TIPS
Spring-creek character — clear, slow, spooky fish. Go long and light, and match tiny midges and baetis. Several stretches border working ranches along Benton Crossing Road; stay in the public corridor.
BENTON · HIGH DESERT

BENTON HOT SPRINGS PONDS

  • sunfish
  • small bass
  • catfish
BEST SEASON — spring through fall

BAIT & GEAR. small worms, nightcrawlers, and tiny jigs

NOTES & TIPS
Warm-water trickles and ranch ponds — a fun contrast to the trout water and rarely crowded. Ask before fishing private ponds; most owners are friendly. Overcast days fish best here.

MARKLEEVILLE → MINDEN · ALPINE COUNTY

EAST FORK CARSON RIVER — UPPER

  • rainbow trout
  • brown trout
  • lahontan cutthroat
  • whitefish
BEST SEASON — spring & fall; summer mornings and evenings

BAIT & GEAR. nymphs, caddis, stoneflies, and PMDs; ultralight spinners or worms

NOTES & TIPS
Partly wild-trout and special-regulation water — check current CDFW/NDOW regs before you go. Runoff runs high into June. Rocky wading; studded boots are worth it.
GARDEN VALLEY → CARSON CITY

EAST FORK CARSON RIVER — LOWER

  • rainbow trout
  • brown trout
  • smallmouth bass
BEST SEASON — spring through fall

BAIT & GEAR. small jigs, worms, and spinners; fly rods for the trout

NOTES & TIPS
Bigger water with more structure and more pressure. Access off Hwy 88 and 395; summer smallmouth hold in the deeper pools below the rapids. Flows change fast below diversions — check before wading.
CA–NV BORDER · 395 CORRIDOR

TOPAZ LAKE

  • rainbow trout
  • brown trout
BEST SEASON — spring through fall; early spring right after plants

BAIT & GEAR. trolling, worms, and powerBait-style baits; jigs and small spinners

NOTES & TIPS
State-line lake — carry the license for the side you fish. Two resorts with launches and rentals. Afternoon wind is relentless, so go early; browns cruise the north end once fall arrives.
CARSON CITY · URBAN CORRIDOR

CARSON RIVER — CARSON CITY STRETCH

  • rainbow trout
  • smallmouth & largemouth bass
  • channel catfish
  • bullhead
BEST SEASON — year-round; trout stocked spring & fall, bass in summer

BAIT & GEAR. worms, powerBait, and shrimp for trout; jigs and soft plastics for bass; stink bait for catfish

NOTES & TIPS
Several parks give easy public access right through town. Late-summer flows drop hard — fish the deeper runs and bridge pools. Catfish bite best at night near the bridges.
CARSON VALLEY · EAST FORK OXBOWS

EAST FORK SIDE CHANNELS & TRIBUTARIES

  • rainbow trout
  • small bass
  • panfish
BEST SEASON — spring & fall

BAIT & GEAR. small worms, tiny jigs, and light-tackle spinners

NOTES & TIPS
Quiet oxbows and side sloughs off the main fork — kayak or short walk-in. Spring runoff reconnects them and pulls fish in. Easy bank spots for a quick evening session after work.

WADSWORTH · PYRAMID LAKE PAIUTE TRIBE

PYRAMID LAKE

  • lahontan cutthroat trout
  • mackinaw
  • tui chub
BEST SEASON — spring & fall; early season right after plants

BAIT & GEAR. trolling spoons and dodgers; powerBait-style baits from shore

NOTES & TIPS
Tribal permit required — buy it before you wet a line. Famous ladder fishing early season for giant cutthroat. Wind is brutal and dangerous; check the forecast and fish the lee shore. Macks run deep later in the year.
LAKE TAHOE EAST SHORE · NEAR SPARKS

SPOONER LAKE

  • rainbow trout
  • smallmouth bass
BEST SEASON — spring through fall

BAIT & GEAR. ultralight spinning gear, small spinners and worms; light fly rod

NOTES & TIPS
State park access with a day-use fee; hike-in shoreline, no gas motors. Smallmouth cruise the shallows on calm mornings. Best before the summer beach crowds arrive.
LAKE TAHOE BASIN · CARSON CITY BACKCOUNTRY

MARLETTE LAKE & HOBART RESERVOIR

  • rainbow trout
  • kokanee
  • small bass
BEST SEASON — summer through early fall

BAIT & GEAR. trolling rigs and small spinners; light fly-fishing for trout

NOTES & TIPS
Hike-in or rough 4wd backcountry lakes — snow locks the roads until July. Gin-clear water: fish light, go early, and pack everything in. Kokanee show in Marlette by mid-summer.
